01 PROFILE & PHILOSOPHY

Hello, I'm Priyanka.

Visual Designer, UI/UX Crafter & Storyteller based in Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh.

I am a creative and detail-oriented Graphic Designer and UI/UX Designer with a deep passion for visual communication, modern branding, digital interfaces, and thoughtful typography.

Equipped with a solid foundation in digital media through my DGWA (Diploma in Graphics, Web Design & 2D Animation) at MAAC Gorakhpur and an analytical background from my B.Sc. at Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Gorakhpur University, I merge creative intuition with methodical structure.

Whether designing an artisanal identity for a cafe, engineering a multi-device UI system, crafting high-impact advertising posters, or creating expressive vector illustrations, my aim is always simple: craft work that communicates clearly, connects emotionally, and leaves an enduring impression.

06 METHODOLOGY

How I Design

A disciplined, 5-stage creative process that transforms ambiguous briefs into purposeful visual solutions.

01

Discover & Immerse

Unpacking the brief, defining audience psychology, analyzing competitive context, and curating an inspiring moodboard of textures, typography, and color aesthetics.

Brief Analysis Visual Moodboards Context Research
02

Define & Wireframe

Structuring the information architecture. Rapid thumbnail sketches for posters and brand marks; low-fidelity wireframes and user flow mapping for UI/UX projects.

Thumbnail Sketches UX Architecture Low-Fi Layouts
03

Design & Compose

Translating structural wireframes into high-fidelity visual artwork. Crafting custom typography pairings, precise vector curves in Illustrator, and cohesive color systems.

Figma UI Systems Vector Geometry Color Balancing
04

Refine & Contextualize

Placing designs into realistic multi-device frames, print packaging mockups, and real-world billboard settings. Rigorous micro-detail audits and typography adjustments.

3D Device Mockups Packaging Renders Hierarchy Audit
05

Deliver & Document

Exporting print-ready high-resolution files (CMYK 300 DPI, bleed specs) for physical collateral, and responsive SVG assets, design tokens, and components for developers.

Print-Ready CMYK Developer Handoff Asset Organization
